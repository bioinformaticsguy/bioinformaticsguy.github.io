How shared cohort candidates are selected and ordered

These are recurrent cohort candidates, not individual diagnostic SNV tiers. It identifies rare, high-CADD variants observed in multiple members of this cohort; it does not use phenotype, pedigree, inheritance, or a gene panel.

  1. Eligibility: a non-reference genotype in at least 2 samples.
  2. Technical filter: VCF FILTER is PASS or ..
  3. Population rarity: gnomAD AF is present and ≤ 0.001.
  4. Predicted impact: CADD PHRED is ≥ 25.
  5. Ordering: more carriers first, then pathogenic/likely-pathogenic ClinVar and/or LoF VEP evidence, then higher CADD PHRED, then genomic coordinate to break any remaining tie.

How cohort SV evidence tiers are assigned
  1. Tier 1: rare pathogenic ClinVar SV with GOI-gene involvement, or a rare high-CADD-SV call overlapping/very close to a GOI gene.
  2. Tier 2: rare moderate-CADD-SV calls overlapping/very close to GOI genes, or rare high-CADD-SV calls close/far from GOI genes.
  3. Tier 3: rare moderate-CADD-SV calls close/far from GOI genes, or rare lower-score calls with GOI involvement.
  4. Tier 4: rare non-GOI pathogenic ClinVar or high/moderate-CADD-SV exonic/intronic calls.
